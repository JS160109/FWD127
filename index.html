About the Project

What is SysGrid?

A simple web page, made with only HTML and CSS, that replaces the paper register in the computer lab. Students pick a PC, report faulty machines and log their session.

Problems it solves

  • Long queues at the paper register
  • Sitting at a PC that has a broken mouse or no network
  • No easy way to check who used which PC
